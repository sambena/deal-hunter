Query tips

Every word must be in the listing title. a|b means either one. "exact phrase" in quotes. -word excludes. Matching ignores case, dashes and spaces, so 10980xe finds "i9-10980 XE". Model numbers work better than full product names. Junk like "for parts" or "bent pins" is always filtered (edit the list in Settings).

Your watches

Add your machines and their parts, then press Find upgrades. Built-in rules recognise desktop platforms from about 2015 on and suggest drop-in CPUs and RAM. With AI on, you also get GPU/storage ideas and support for anything the rules don't know.
